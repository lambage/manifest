## SUBSTITUTIONS & ALTERNATIVES

### Frame Material Alternatives
- **2"x2" Aluminum** → 2"x2" Steel (heavier, cheaper)
- **2"x2" Aluminum** → 80/20 T-slot extrusion (easier assembly)
- **Square Tube** → Round tube (slightly weaker in bending)

### Electronics Alternatives
- **ESP32** → Arduino + WiFi shield (more complex)
- **FSR 402** → Piezo sensors (different signal processing)
- **LiPo Battery** → 18650 cell pack (requires BMS)

### Padding Alternatives
- **HD Foam** → EVA foam mats (cheaper, less durable)
- **Vinyl Cover** → Canvas duck cloth (more breathable)
- **Plywood** → HDPE plastic sheet (weather resistant)
