## Key Specifications (All Builds)

### Physical Dimensions
- **Base**: 48" x 48" (122cm x 122cm)
- **Height**: 72-84" (183-213cm) adjustable
- **Weight**: 150-300 lbs depending on build
- **Clearance Required**: 6 feet (2m) radius minimum

### Target Positions (6 total)
- **Head**: 66-70" high, center
- **Chest**: 48-52" high, center
- **Left Arm**: 48-52" high, 14" left
- **Right Arm**: 48-52" high, 14" right
- **Left Kick**: 24-30" high, 12" left
- **Right Kick**: 24-30" high, 12" right

### Performance Requirements
- **Max Impact Force**: 1,000 lbs (4.4 kN)
- **User Weight**: Up to 250 lbs (113 kg)
- **Stability**: No tipping under 100 lbs at 45° angle
- **Adjustment Range**: 4-6.5 feet (1.2-2m)
